Transparency & Accountability

Trust is the foundation of meaningful change

The Diane and Wilner Foundation believes that the people we serve, our donors, volunteers and community partners deserve transparency about who we are and how we operate. We are committed to responsible stewardship, accountability and using the resources entrusted to us to advance our charitable mission.

Financial transparency

How we account for what we're given

We are a small, founder-led foundation, and we would rather tell you exactly where we are than publish numbers that flatter us.

We file annually with the IRS as required for an organization of our size, using Form 990, 990-EZ or 990-N as applicable.

We publish no impact statistics, cost-per-child figures, or program-percentage claims that we cannot document. When we can document them, we will publish them here.

Our financial information reported to the State of Florida is available to anyone through the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.
