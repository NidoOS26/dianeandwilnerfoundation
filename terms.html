Making a gift

At this time, this website does not process donations online. If you'd like to give, please use our contact form, and our team will follow up personally with secure ways to give. When you make a gift to Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc., you are giving to a tax-exempt nonprofit organization recognized under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 87-3797225; Florida Charitable Registration No. CH67202) in support of our mission for underserved children and families, including our work connected to Haiti.

Contributions to Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc. are tax deductible to the extent permitted by law under Section 170 of the Internal Revenue Code. We will provide a written acknowledgement for your gift; please retain it for your tax records. We are not able to give tax advice, so please consult your own tax advisor about your particular situation.

We do not promise that a specific percentage of your gift will go to any particular program unless we have shared verified financial information stating so.

Tax deductibility

Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc. is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a tax-exempt public charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, with public charity status under IRC 170(b)(1)(A)(vi).
