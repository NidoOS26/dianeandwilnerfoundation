Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where does my gift go?

Your gift is directed toward the foundation's core work: school supplies and backpacks for Backpack Day, healthcare access for sponsored children, and rebuilding safe homes and orphanage care in Haiti. As we grow, we're building out clearer program-level reporting so every dollar is traceable to a specific child, home, or classroom.

Is the foundation founder-funded?

Yes — the Diane & Wilner Foundation began with Wilner Frage personally funding the mission, including the full support of two children in a Haitian orphanage. We're now inviting the diaspora and wider community to join that commitment.

Can my company donate supplies instead of money?

Yes. In-kind donations of backpacks, school supplies, and medical supplies are always welcome, especially ahead of the next Backpack Day. Reach out through the contact form below.

How is the foundation different from other charities?

We are diaspora-led and locally connected — a bridge between people with resources and children who need stability, led by a founder who has already proven his commitment with his own money and time on the ground in Haiti.
