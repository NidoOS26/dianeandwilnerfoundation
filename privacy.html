Privacy Policy

How Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc. collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 87-3797225) based at 157 Tulpan Drive, Kissimmee, Florida 34743, United States. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we handle it.

Information you may share with us

We collect information only when you choose to give it to us. Depending on how you interact with this site, that may include:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and any message you send us, including inquiries about giving, sponsorship, volunteering, or partnership.
  • Volunteer or partnership inquiries: the same contact details above, submitted through our contact form.
  • Donation-related communication: at this time, this website does not process online payments. If you reach out about giving, we collect only what you provide in your message so our team can follow up directly with secure ways to give.
  • Newsletter or email sign-up: if we add a sign-up option in the future, we will collect only the email address (and, if requested, name) you provide.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect sensitive information such as government ID numbers, financial account numbers, or payment card details through this website.

How we use this information

We use the information you share to respond to your inquiry, coordinate volunteering or partnership opportunities, follow up on a gift you'd like to make, and keep you informed about our work if you've asked to hear from us. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Cookies and website analytics

This website does not currently use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools to monitor visitors. If that changes in the future — for example, to understand which pages are most helpful to visitors — we will update this policy to describe what is used and how you can control it.

Third-party payment processors

This website does not currently process donations online, and no third-party payment processor is connected to this site. If online giving is enabled in the future, payments will be handled by a reputable third-party payment processor, and Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc. will not store your credit card or bank account details on this website. We will update this policy to name that processor and explain how it handles your information before online giving goes live.

How we protect and share information

Information submitted through our contact form is used only by Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc. and the people who help run it. We do not share your personal information with outside organizations except when necessary to respond to your request (for example, connecting you with our team about a gift or volunteer opportunity), or when required by law.

Photos, videos, and stories

Photos and videos shared on this website depicting children and families are used with the appropriate permission of a parent, guardian, or local partner, and are used to tell the story of our work honestly and with dignity. If you have a concern about an image or story involving you or a family member, please contact us and we will address it.

How long we keep information

We keep the information you submit for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry or maintain our relationship with you as a donor, volunteer, or partner, and we remove it when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our contact form or any other feature of this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and programs evolve. We will post any changes here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our contact page, or write to us at Diane and Wilner Foundation, Inc., 157 Tulpan Drive, Kissimmee, Florida 34743, United States.
